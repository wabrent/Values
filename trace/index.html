Three checks. One command.

When you download a model, Trace runs three independent verification layers.

01

Hash verification

Computes SHA-256 of every weight file, compares against a public integrity registry. If the hash matches, the bytes you downloaded are the bytes that were published.

02

Provenance check

Reads the model card, training metadata, and license. Flags missing information. You should know who trained it, on what data, and what changed along the way.

03

Behavioral smoke test

Runs 5 standard prompts and compares the output against reference responses. Detects malicious fine-tuning, backdoors, and accidental corruption that hash verification alone would miss.

Why this doesn't exist yet.

Everyone downloads models blindly. No one verifies them. That is a supply chain attack waiting to happen.

For developers

Verify before you build

Before integrating a model into your product, run Trace. Get a supply chain report in HTML. Share it with your team. Build on verified foundations.
