How it works

One file. Every model. No retraining.

Values asks you real questions — not "do you value honesty?" but "should I tell you your poem is mediocre when you're proud of it?" — and turns your answers into a portable file that literally changes how any AI responds to you.

01

Answer dilemmas

13 real situations. No abstract questions. Every one forces a trade-off between two defensible behaviours. 6 axes: care, truth, autonomy, privacy, risk, agency.

~10 min · terminal or web
02

Get your file

Your answers become a signed .ethos JSON. Read it. Edit it. It's yours. SHA-256 proves nobody tampered; Ed25519 proves it's from you.

portable · human-readable · tamper-evident
03

Compile for any model

One command turns your file into a system prompt, an Ollama Modelfile, an Anthropic skill, or a JSON message. Works on any instruction-following model.

llama · gemma · qwen · claude · gpt
04

Prove it works

The probe engine runs every dilemma twice — with and without your file — and measures the difference. Numbers, not promises. Delta is the headline.

measurable · transparent · not magic
The problem

900 million people use AI weekly.
Every answer follows someone else's rules.

A lab in California decides what every model says, what it refuses, what it hides. Your culture, your faith, your beliefs — none of it matters to the system prompt. Billions of people share one set of defaults they never chose.

If you ask ChatGPT to push back on bad decisions

It won't. It's trained to defer. No setting changes that — not in your account, not in Custom Instructions, not anywhere. That's not a bug. It's a choice someone else made.

If you want Claude to correct your mistakes mid-task

It won't, unless you ask. It's trained to stay on task. That is a value judgment — "don't interrupt" is not neutral — and it was made without you in the room.

Values isn't about making AI "safer." It's about making AI yours. The file is yours. The probe is evidence. Nothing leaves your machine.

Proof, not promises

A file that claims to change behaviour must prove it.

Every dilemma runs twice against the same model: bare, then with your values. The probe classifies responses, counts flips, and gives you a number. Not a confidence score from a black box — a measurement you can inspect.

When two values collide, the earlier one wins.
